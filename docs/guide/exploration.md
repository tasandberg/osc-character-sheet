# Exploration checks

Exploration checks are rolled on a d6: you succeed if you roll your chance or lower, so "2-in-6" succeeds on a 1 or 2. For the rules, see the [Old-School Essentials SRD](https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/).

## Where are the exploration checks?

- In a narrow or medium-width window: on the **Actions** tab, under **Exploration**.
- In a wide window: in the left column, under the **Exploration** tab next to **Saves**.

The sheet lists six checks, each with your chance:

- **Listen at Door**
- **Open Stuck Door**
- **Find Secret Door**
- **Find Trap**
- **Forage**
- **Hunt**

![Exploration checks](images/exploration-checks.png)

## How do I roll a check?

Click it. The sheet hands the roll to OSE, which treats exploration checks as blind rolls: skip the dialog (Ctrl-click, or Cmd-click on a Mac) and only the GM sees the result.

If your version of OSE doesn't store one of these checks, the sheet keeps your chance for it and rolls a plain 1d6 to chat in your current roll mode.

## How do I change my chances?

Click **Edit** in the top bar. In the **Exploration** section of the Edit Character window, choose a new chance (**1-in-6** to **6-in-6**) for each check. Changes save straight away.
