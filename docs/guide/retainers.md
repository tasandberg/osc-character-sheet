# Retainers

A retainer uses the same sheet as any other character, with a few extras. For the rules on hiring and loyalty, see the [Old-School Essentials SRD](https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/).

## How do I make a character a retainer?

1. Open the retainer's sheet and click **Edit** in the top bar.
2. In the Edit Character window, tick **Retainer**.
3. Fill in the fields that appear: **Wage**, **Loyalty Rating** and, optionally, **Employer**.

The sheet's accent color switches from brass to teal so you can tell retainers apart at a glance.

![Retainer sheet with the loyalty and wage row](images/retainers-sheet.png)

## How do I set loyalty?

Enter a **Loyalty Rating** from 0 to 12 in the Edit Character window.

If you pick an **Employer**, the window shows the loyalty that employer's Charisma gives ("Value based on employer's CHA"). If you've entered something different, click that default to reset to it.

The **Employer** list shows the player-owned characters in the world. Choosing one is optional.

## Where do I see loyalty and wage?

At the top of the **Actions** tab, a **Retainer** row shows **Loyalty** and **Wage**. **Wage** shows a dash until it's set. It's free text, so write it however your table tracks pay, for example "25 gp/month".

## How do I roll loyalty?

Click the **Loyalty** number. OSE rolls 2d6 against the Loyalty Rating and posts the result to chat.

## How do I stop a character being a retainer?

Untick **Retainer** in the Edit Character window. The **Retainer** row disappears and the accent goes back to brass.
