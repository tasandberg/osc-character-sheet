# Ability scores and saving throws

For the rules behind checks and saves, see the [Old-School Essentials SRD](https://oldschoolessentials.necroticgnome.com/srd/).

## Where are my ability scores?

At the top of the **Actions** tab, as six plaques: **STR**, **INT**, **WIS**, **DEX**, **CON** and **CHA**. Each shows the score with its modifier beneath. The modifiers come from OSE, so they always match the system's own sheet.

![Ability score plaques](images/abilities-plaques.png)

## How do I change a score?

Click **Edit** in the top bar and use the **Ability Scores** section of the Edit Character window. Scores run from 1 to 20. If a score is below your class's minimum, it's flagged with "needs N+".

## How do I roll an ability check?

Click the ability's plaque. OSE opens its roll dialog and posts the result to chat, using its roll-under check against the score.

To skip the dialog, hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) while you click. If your GM has turned on OSE's **Invert Roll Ctrl/Meta-key** setting, it works the other way round: a plain click skips the dialog and a Ctrl-click shows it. This applies to every roll the sheet hands to OSE: ability checks, saves, exploration checks and the **Attack** button.

## Where are my saving throws?

- In a narrow or medium-width window: on the **Actions** tab, under **Saving Throws**.
- In a wide window: in the left column, under the **Saves** tab.

Each save shows your target number: **Death**, **Wand**, **Paralysis**, **Breath** and **Spell**. You succeed by rolling that number or higher on a d20.

![Saving throws on the Actions tab](images/abilities-saves.png)

## How do I roll a saving throw?

Click the save. OSE opens its save dialog and posts the result to chat. Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on a Mac) skips the dialog, as above.

## What is the "vs Magic" tag?

If your Wisdom modifier isn't zero, a tag next to your saves shows it, for example **+1 vs Magic**. To add it to a save against a magical effect, click **vs Magic** in OSE's save dialog instead of the normal roll button. Skipping the dialog rolls without it. Hover the tag to see your Wisdom score.

## How do I change my save targets?

Use the **Saving Throws** section of the Edit Character window. Once your character has a class, each save shows its class default underneath; if you change a value, click the default to reset it.
