The Inventory tab lists everything your character carries.

## Encumbrance

The readout above the load bar shows your three movement rates and your total load in coins (cn). Rates drop as the load crosses each threshold.

| Load (cn) | Movement |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 400 | Full |
| Up to 600 | Three quarters |

## Equipping items

Equip a weapon or armour to use it in the Actions tab.
